How to play

🎯Objective: race to Floor 6 and defeat the Sovereign, or hold the strongest position when your friends call the game.
⚔️Turn actions (2 AP): Hunt, Train, Raid, Sabotage, and Ascend each cost 1 action point. Trade, Craft, and Transmute are free.
🎲Hunt: roll 2d6 vs the floor's DR. Success grants +1 progress and a Loot Card, usually this floor's material, sometimes Broken Gear (single use, +2 to a Hunt — equip it and a checkbox lets you decide which Hunt to spend it on, it's never forced). A natural 2 raids whichever rival shares your floor instead of costing you progress — only an empty floor (or a Shield) spares you. Double sixes crit for +2 progress, two Loot Cards, and a free action. When a roll draws more than one Loot Card, you pick which one to keep.
📚Train: spend 2 of the current floor's material for a guaranteed +1 progress, no roll. The reliable way to climb when the dice aren't cooperating.
🗡️Raid: pick a target and steal 1 random material. Both sides then roll 2d6 plus gear bonuses (basic gear +2, upgraded +3, an equipped accessory +1); higher score lands it, ties favor the defender. Broken Gear stays Hunt-only and never adds to a Raid roll. Go all-in to wager a material: win, and you keep it plus knock the target down 1 progress; lose, and they keep your stake.
🤝Trade: a want-board, not a one-to-one proposal. Post what material you need, and any guild can pitch what they'd take for it. Accept whichever offer you like, decline one without cancelling your want, or cancel the want outright. One active want per guild at a time, free to post, pitch, decline, or accept.
🪄Sabotage: spend 1 material to curse a rival, their next Hunt total takes -1. Go all-in to spend 2 instead: the curse doubles to -2, and they lose 1 progress immediately.
🏹Blacksmith: craft Basic Bow (2 Kasaka Fang, reroll a die) or Basic Sword (2 Golem Crystal, +2 total), upgrade them, or craft accessories: Shield (blocks a natural-2 penalty), Lucky Coin (+1 loot on success), Compass (+1 progress on success), Ironclad Ward (blocks the next Raid against you outright). 3 item slots, only 1 accessory at a time.
✨Shadow Transmutation: discard any 3 materials, in any mix, to receive 1 material of your choice. Free, and repeatable as long as you have the materials and a slot for the result.
🪜Ascend: 1 AP. Once you meet the progress requirement and hold the toll material, move up to the next floor. Floors 3, 5 and 6 also need a Floor Key: Vanguard's Sigil (1 Kasaka Fang + 1 Golem Crystal), Kaisel Ward (1 Orc Tusk + 1 Oracle Wisp), Sovereign's Bane (1 Kaisel Scale + 1 Kasaka Fang). A Key is crafted and spent in the same instant, so it never takes a slot, and the last one reaches back to Floor 1 on purpose.
🧺Storage: 4 material slots and 3 item slots. The cap is on variety, never on how many copies of one material you stack. Only 1 accessory at a time.
🧹Scavenge: 1 AP. Shows up when you have no material options — can't Train, Blacksmith, Transmute, Ascend, or Trade with what you're holding. Take 1 material from this floor's pool, or the nearest lower one. Once per turn.
🏁Ending: defeat the Sovereign on Floor 6 to win outright, or anyone can hit End game to call it whenever. Standings then rank by floor reached, then progress on that floor, then the value of materials and gear on hand (a material is worth its floor number).
💀Monster Outbreak: the lowest floor nobody has cleared yet carries a countdown, shown above the Tower panel. It resets when that floor is finally cleared. If it hits 0, the guild furthest ahead loses 2 progress and everyone else loses 1.
💥Camping: stay on the same floor past 3 of your own turns and the Monster attacks, -1 progress and -1 material.
🍀Tower Events: roughly 1 in 3 turns draws a random event, good or bad, when your turn begins.
📈Catch-up: whoever is on the lowest floor gets +1 added to their Hunt rolls.
