We can walk you through it as you go — short tips will pop up near whatever you're about to do, the first time you do it. Nothing forced, nothing to memorize up front.

How to play

Objective: race to Floor 6 and defeat the Sovereign, or hold the strongest position when your friends call the game.
Turn actions (2 AP): Hunt, Train, Raid, Sabotage, and Ascend each cost 1 action point. Trade, Craft, and Transmute are free.
Hunt: roll 2d6 vs the floor's DR. Bow and Sword are permanent weapons and always apply. Shield, Lucky Coin, Compass, and Broken Gear are single use — a checkbox lets you decide which Hunt to spend one on, it's never forced, and it breaks once it actually triggers. Success grants +1 progress and a Loot Card, usually this floor's material. A natural 2 costs you 1 banked Ascension Progress unless a checked Shield spares you. Double sixes crit for +2 progress, two Loot Cards, and a free action. When a roll draws more than one Loot Card, you pick which one to keep.
Train: spend 2 of the current floor's material for a guaranteed +1 progress, no roll. The reliable way to climb when the dice aren't cooperating.
Raid: pick a target and steal 1 random material. Both sides then roll their own 2d6 — gear doesn't factor in here, it's a pure roll-off; higher score lands it, ties favor the defender. Go all-in to wager a material: win, and you keep it plus knock the target down 1 progress; lose, and they keep your stake.
Trade: a want-board, not a one-to-one proposal. Post what material you need, and any guild can pitch what they'd take for it. Accept whichever offer you like, decline one without cancelling your want, or cancel the want outright. One active want per guild at a time, free to post, pitch, decline, or accept.
Sabotage: spend 1 material to try to curse a rival — both sides roll their own 2d6 (gear doesn't factor in), and they resist it on a tie or better. Land it, and their next Hunt total takes -2. Go all-in to spend 2 instead: land it for a -3 curse and an immediate 1 progress loss.
Blacksmith: craft Basic Bow (2 Kasaka Fang, reroll a die) or Basic Sword (2 Golem Crystal, +2 total) — permanent weapons, upgrade them for a bigger bonus — or craft an accessory: Shield (blocks a natural-2 penalty), Lucky Coin (+1 loot on success), Compass (+1 progress on success), Ironclad Ward (blocks the next Raid against you outright). Accessories are single use, then they break. 3 item slots, only 1 accessory at a time.
Shadow Transmutation: discard any 3 materials, in any mix, to receive 1 material of your choice. Free, and repeatable as long as you have the materials and a slot for the result.
Ascend: 1 AP. Once you meet the progress requirement and hold the toll material, move up to the next floor. Floors 3, 5 and 6 also need a Floor Key: Vanguard's Sigil (1 Kasaka Fang + 1 Golem Crystal), Kaisel Ward (1 Orc Tusk + 1 Oracle Wisp), Sovereign's Bane (1 Kaisel Scale + 1 Kasaka Fang). A Key is crafted and spent in the same instant, so it never takes a slot, and the last one reaches back to Floor 1 on purpose.
Storage: 3 material slots and 3 item slots. The cap is on variety, never on how many copies of one material you stack. Only 1 accessory at a time.
Scavenge: 1 AP. Shows up when you have no material options — can't Train, Blacksmith, Transmute, Ascend, or Trade with what you're holding. Take 1 material from this floor's pool, or the nearest lower one. Once per turn.
Ending: defeat the Sovereign on Floor 6 to win outright, or anyone can hit End game to call it whenever. Standings then rank by floor reached, then progress on that floor, then the value of materials and gear on hand (a material is worth its floor number).
Monster Outbreak: the lowest floor nobody has cleared yet carries a countdown, shown above the Tower panel. If it hits 0, the guild furthest ahead loses 1 progress and 1 material, while everyone else loses a material, or 1 progress if they're holding nothing — and it stays active, hitting the table again every round, until someone actually ascends (any floor, any guild) to drive it off. The higher you've climbed, the more it costs you. If everyone happens to be tied on the same floor when it fires, there's no one it can single out for the usual hit — instead, whoever's leading on that floor (the top guild, or top two in a 4-player game) only gets 1 AP on their turn instead of 2, every round, until someone ascends and breaks up the tie.
Camping: stay on the same floor past 4 of your own turns and the Monster comes for you — roll 1d6, a 4 or higher fights it off, anything lower costs you 1 random material. The check comes round again every 4 turns you stay put.
Tower Events: roughly 1 in 3 turns draws a random event, good or bad, when your turn begins.
Catch-up: if you're 2 or more floors behind whoever's closest ahead of you, you get +1 added to your Hunt rolls.
